Why this is the right cut

A catalog should load in Claude, Cursor, or Grok without buying CrossR process. A process should compose catalog skills as nodes without owning their text. That's already the stratified-disclosure design, physically violated by stuffing everything in one tree. After the split, HARNESS-SPEC §12–13 defining AVRIL/AXEL is a spec bug: loop law lives in loops, artifact/ritual law in harness, skill law in SKILL.md, topology in graphs/ (map, not executor).
